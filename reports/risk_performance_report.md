# Risk and Performance Report

## Measures

Daily simple returns use consecutive observed `EQ` closes. Annualized volatility and Sharpe use 252 sessions per year; the Sharpe calculation assumes a zero risk-free rate. Downside deviation is the annualized root-mean-square of negative daily returns. These assumptions are methodological choices, not source metadata.

## Findings

Across the selected stocks, ICICIBANK has the highest annualized return (18.04%) over its available period, while TATAMOTORS has the highest annualized volatility (43.34%). Estimates are not corporate-action adjusted and should not be read as total-return or investment conclusions.

The highest observed monthly return is TATAMOTORS in 2019-10 (51.30%); the lowest is RELIANCE in 2024-10 (-54.89%). ICICIBANK has the highest arithmetic average daily return in the selected sample (0.08%).
